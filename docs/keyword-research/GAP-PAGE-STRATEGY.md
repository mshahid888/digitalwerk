## 3. Local / Ansbach Opportunity Analysis (expanded)

This expands the "Local (Ansbach) coverage is currently thin everywhere"
observation from `KEYWORD-STRATEGY.md` into a full analysis across every
tier, plus a technical-SEO finding uncovered while researching this task.

### 3.1 What local signal currently exists on the site (verified from source)

- `lib/site-config.ts` holds a **real, confirmed physical address**:
  `Martin-Luther-Platz 14, 91522 Ansbach` (`addressConfirmed: true`).
- `lib/schema.ts`'s `buildOrganizationJsonLd()` emits this address in
  `PostalAddress`, but **only `streetAddress` and `addressCountry: "DE"`**
  — there is no `addressLocality` ("Ansbach"), `addressRegion` ("Bayern"),
  `postalCode` ("91522"), or `GeoCoordinates` anywhere in the schema.
- `areaServed` on both the Organization and every Service schema is
  hardcoded to `{"@type": "Country", name: "Germany"}` — a Germany-wide
  signal only, with no city- or region-level local signal layered on top.
- `siteConfig.social` (Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, WhatsApp) does
  **not** include a Google Business Profile / Google Maps listing URL for
  DigitalWerk's own presence — notable since `/loesungen/google-
  unternehmensprofil` is a page where DigitalWerk sells GBP optimization
  *to clients*, but doesn't reference its own.
- No dedicated local-landing page exists for Ansbach or Mittelfranken.

**This is a real, evidenced gap — not a new page-creation opportunity, a
schema/technical one.** Flagging it here because it surfaced directly from
this task's local-keyword research; not implemented in this document (see
§4 for what happens with it next).

### 3.2 What the actual keyword data says about local demand

| SEO | `seo agentur ansbach` | **REAL — 30/mo, CPC $8.07** (the only *measured* local number in the entire project) |
| SEO | `SEO Ansbach`, `Suchmaschinenoptimierung Ansbach` | RECOVERED — not measured |
| SEO (regional) | `SEO Nürnberg`, `SEO Fürth`, `SEO Rothenburg`, `SEO Mittelfranken` | RECOVERED — not measured |
| Webdesign | `Webdesign Ansbach`, `Website Agentur Ansbach` | RECOVERED — not measured |
| Webentwicklung | `Webentwicklung Ansbach` | RECOVERED — not measured |
| Digital Marketing | `Marketing Agentur Ansbach`, `Online Marketing Ansbach`, `Digital Marketing Bayern` | RECOVERED — not measured |
| Social & Content | `Social Media Marketing Ansbach` | RECOVERED — not measured |
| KI-Agenten | `ki agenten ansbach` | NEW_GENERATED — unmeasured candidate |
| KI-Automatisierung | `ki automatisierung ansbach` | NEW_GENERATED — unmeasured candidate |
| E-Commerce | `e-commerce agentur ansbach` | NEW_GENERATED — unmeasured candidate |
| **Leadgenerierung** | `leadgenerierung ansbach` | NEW_GENERATED — unmeasured candidate; **this tier had zero local term of any kind before this task** |

**Reading of the evidence:** every *measured* local number found so far is
small (30/mo is the only one that exists at all) — consistent with Ansbach
being a small town (~40,000 population). This is real evidence against
building multiple thin, dedicated per-keyword local landing pages: the
volume doesn't support it. It's real evidence *for* concentrating local
signal on a small number of channels that don't require new pages: schema,
Google Business Profile, and local-intent copy embedded within the
existing/planned service pages.

### 3.3 Recommended local-SEO approach (proposal, not implemented)

1. **Schema enhancement** (technical, no new page): add `addressLocality:
   "Ansbach"`, `addressRegion: "Bayern"`, `postalCode: "91522"` to
   `PostalAddress` in `buildOrganizationJsonLd()`, and consider adding
   `GeoCoordinates` for Ansbach. Consider layering a city/region-level
   `areaServed` (Ansbach + Mittelfranken + Bayern) alongside the existing
   Germany-wide one, rather than replacing it — DigitalWerk genuinely
   serves clients beyond Ansbach, so this should add local specificity
   without narrowing the existing national claim.
2. **Google Business Profile**: create/confirm one for DigitalWerk's own
   listing and add its URL to `sameAs`. (Outside this document's/this
   session's authority to create the listing itself — that's an external
   account action, not a code change.)
3. **In-page local copy, not new pages**: fold `Marketing Agentur Ansbach`,
   `Webdesign Ansbach`, `Webentwicklung Ansbach`, and the Mittelfranken/
   Nürnberg/Fürth/Rothenburg regional terms into the "Why Choose
   DigitalWerk" sections of the *existing* relevant pages
   (`/loesungen/seo`, `/loesungen/webentwicklung`, and the two new gap
   pages once built) rather than creating standalone local landing pages —
   the measured volume doesn't justify page-level fragmentation.
4. **The one exception to "no new local pages" worth flagging for a future
   decision** (not proposed for immediate action): if `seo agentur
   ansbach` and its regional siblings ever justify a single consolidated
   "SEO Agentur Ansbach & Mittelfranken" landing page, that would be the
   one candidate — a single hub rather than one page per city.

None of §3.3 is implemented in this session. It's recorded here as the
proposed next local-SEO step for your review, consistent with "do not
create pages yet."
